Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I restore my VIP Pro purchase?

Open the app, navigate to the Profile (Hồ sơ) tab, tap on VIP Pro Upgrade, and select Khôi phục gói mua (Restore Purchases). If your Apple ID or Google Play account made an active subscription or lifetime purchase, it will automatically restore immediately.

2. How do I cancel a recurring subscription?

Subscriptions can be managed directly on your device:

  • iOS (Apple App Store): Settings > [Your Apple ID] > Subscriptions > Select Verb360 AI > Cancel Subscription.
  • Android (Google Play): Google Play Store app > Profile icon > Payments & Subscriptions > Subscriptions > Select Verb360 AI > Cancel.

3. How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your cloud account and all associated learning history directly inside the app under Profile settings, or by emailing nguyenvandan868@gmail.com with your registered email address.
